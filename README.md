## 📌 About the Project

M S Enterprises wanted to establish a professional online presence for its housekeeping and facility management services.

I designed and developed a business-focused landing page that presents the company's services, business information, and contact details in a clean and responsive format.

The project also included supporting the company's digital presence through Google Business, LinkedIn content, and marketing creatives.
